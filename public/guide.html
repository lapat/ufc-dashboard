Rule #1 The Two-Tab DraftKings Setup

For Bet Bot to track your bets and capture 1-second odds in real time, you must keep two DraftKings tabs open in Google Chrome simultaneously:

  • Tab 1 — My Bets: Keep open to https://sportsbook.draftkings.com/mybets.
    Why: DraftKings streams your active open bets and settled returns over this tab's WebSocket. The extension auto-refreshes it every 20 seconds so it never goes idle.
  • Tab 2 — Live Fight Odds: Keep open to the specific fight page you are watching (e.g. sportsbook.draftkings.com/event/...).
    Why: The extension automatically scans the live moneyline odds on this screen every 1 second and pushes them to the server.
✓ Zero Button Pressing Required: Odds and bets are captured 100% automatically. You do not need to click "Capture Now".

Dual-Mode Fight Night HUD vs. Classic Dashboard

LiveBetBot has two distinct viewing modes depending on your screen setup:

  • The Fight Night HUD (/hud): Designed to fit in a narrow browser window docked right next to your fight video stream. It shows only the active fight, giant net dollar amounts (+20 vs -10), hedge instructions, and zero clutter.
  • The Classic Dashboard (/): The full desktop command center with historical library archives, game lists, and full position cards.

Both modes run in mathematical lockstep off the exact same backend engine. You can switch between them with one click at any time.

Safety Shield The Stream-Lag / Ghost-Punch Warning

Television broadcasts (ESPN+, Fight Pass, pay-per-view) are typically 15 to 30 seconds behind reality.

DraftKings' sports radar operates with zero latency. When a sudden knockdown or submission occurs inside the cage, DraftKings slashes odds or suspends the market before you see it on TV.

⚠️ When this banner flashes:
⚠️ VIOLENT LINE MOVE (STREAM LAG) — HOLD BETS
Do NOT place a bet. The line moved over 60 points in under 3 seconds. An event already happened in the Octagon that your TV stream has not shown yet. Wait 15 seconds for your broadcast to catch up.

Speed 1-Click Bet Slip Pre-Fill

During live round breaks, hedge opportunities can vanish in 15 seconds. Manually typing stakes on DraftKings takes too long.

  • When a hedge opportunity is live, a gold button appears on LiveBetBot:
    ⚡ 1-Click Pre-fill on DraftKings (5 on Chopurov)
  • Clicking this button tells the extension to switch to your DraftKings odds tab, click the fighter's button to open the betslip, and pre-fill the exact calculated dollar stake.
  • Human Safety First: The extension never clicks "Place Bet" automatically. It outlines the button in glowing green so you can verify the numbers and click it yourself with full confirmation.

Analytics Volatility & Fighter Gas Tanks

The HUD displays four critical metrics for the active matchup:

  • Vol Score: Career volatility rating (how prone this matchup is to wild live odds swings). High score = high hedge potential.
  • Pace (SLpM): Significant strikes landed per minute. Fighters over 5.5 SLpM push extreme volume.
  • TakeDown: Average takedowns landed per 15 minutes.
  • Gas Tank: Automatically tags explosive fighters who fade in rounds 2 and 3 (e.g. "Fast Starter / Fades"), signaling when the live line is likely to reverse.
