# Empirical Crossover Catalyst Findings (Phase 1)

Based on 398 live fight recordings cross-referenced with 2,701 fighter profiles, the base rate for an underdog flipping to a favorite (crossover) was **41.5%**.

## High-Leverage Predictors
When specific matchup conditions are present, the crossover rate jumps significantly:

1. **Striking Defense Disparity (61.5% flip rate):** When an underdog has elite striking defense (> 60%) and the favorite has high damage absorption (SApM > 4.0).
2. **Multi-Catalyst Stacking (62.5% flip rate):** When a fight triggers two or more risk factors for the favorite (e.g., Age >= 35, Reach Disadvantage >= 3.0", Poor StrDef < 55%).

## Moderate / Situational Predictors
- **Physical Disparity:** Underdog has a >= 3.0" reach advantage (**48.3%**).
- **Age Cliff:** Favorite is 35 years or older (**47.6%**).
- **Poor Favorite Defense:** Favorite has StrDef < 55% (**45.8%**).

## Anti-Predictors (Avoid these for crossovers)
- **Grappler vs. Striker Suppression:** When a wrestling-heavy favorite (TDAvg >= 2.5) faces an underdog with poor takedown defense (< 60%), the crossover rate drops to **34.8%**. The favorite typically smothers them, preventing the necessary momentum shift.

## Actionable Strategy for Ish
Focus live betting capital on fights where the favorite has structural vulnerabilities (Age 35+, high absorption, poor defense) and the underdog has a reach advantage or strong defense. Avoid underdog bets when the favorite is a strong wrestler facing poor takedown defense.
